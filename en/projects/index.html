Knowledge systems

LLM/RAG for corporate knowledge that has to be usable in practice.

These systems are valuable only when answers can be verified, cited, and embedded into real workflows. Otherwise they quickly become a polished front-end without practical value.

What matters here is hybrid retrieval, source-linked answers, access control, and a usable internal interface for people who need trustworthy information.

What it demonstrates

  • the ability to design search and answer generation around a specific environment
  • understanding of how answer quality must work together with access control and manageability
  • a focus on usefulness rather than presentation effect

High-risk AI architecture

AI architecture for scenarios where mistakes affect money, risk, and reputation.

These projects usually fail not because of the model, but because business expectations, CISO constraints, data, integrations, and real operating conditions were never reconciled into one workable design.

My role here is to assemble an approach that can be explained, deployed, and supported without turning AI into a new source of risk.

Where this matters most

  • fintech and other regulated environments
  • internal assistants and knowledge-intensive workflows
  • AI readiness, architecture audit, and perimeter choice before launch
